# Realistic EMS v26.0.0b: First Public Beta

Ambulance depots, AMPDS call codes, emergency doctors, emergency corridors and realistic on-scene care for Cities: Skylines II, modelled on Vienna's emergency medical service.

> **Beta:** back up your saves before installing.

## Features

- **Ambulance Depot**: a station without beds that houses many ambulances.
- **Smarter dispatch**: ambulances only take patients to hospitals with a free bed, and only accept new calls once back at their depot.
- **Hospital bed capacity**: configurable in Options.
- **Callsigns and call codes**: unit callsigns and AMPDS call codes (Einsatzcodes Wien) with live call status.
- **Emergency doctor cars (NEF)** from hospitals *(experimental)*.
- **On-scene care time**: police and fire brigade are dispatched for matching codes.
- **Scene parking**: vehicles on scene park off the road, on the sidewalk, in parking lanes or at intersection corners.
- **Emergency driving**: pushing vehicles ahead, lane changes, turning from any lane, clearing red lights, emergency corridors.

Every feature can be switched off in Options. Available in German and English.

## Driving and parking behaviour in this release

- Emergency vehicles stay closer to the middle when passing next to oncoming traffic and no longer squeeze past oncoming trucks.
- Angled parking keeps the rear out of the driving lane and the nose on the pavement.
- A free parking spot is used before the sidewalk.
- Behind slower moving traffic, emergency vehicles overtake on the oncoming lane when it is free. Fire engines do too.
- Pedestrian streets: vehicles stop where they are on scene, with no emergency driving aids there.

## Installation

Subscribe on Paradox Mods (Mod ID `166759`), or copy the mod folder into your CS2 `Mods` directory.

Requires Cities: Skylines II (game version `1.*`).
